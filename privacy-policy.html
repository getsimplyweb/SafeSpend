Privacy Policy
Last updated: May 2026
✓ Affordly collects zero personal data. Everything stays on your device.

1. Who We Are

Affordly is a free personal finance tool that helps you check whether a purchase is affordable based on your monthly income, expenses and savings goal. Affordly is available as a website at affordly.netlify.app and as an Android application.

2. Data We Collect

We collect no personal data whatsoever. Affordly does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any personal information.

All data you enter into Affordly — including your income, expenses, savings goal and purchase history — is stored exclusively in your device's local storage (localStorage) and never transmitted to any server.

3. What Stays on Your Device

  • Monthly income, fixed expenses, savings goal
  • Purchase names and amounts you have checked
  • Affordability scores and EMI calculations
  • Your check history

This data exists only on your device. Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app permanently deletes it. We have no access to it at any time.

4. Cookies & Tracking

Affordly does not use cookies, tracking pixels, fingerprinting, or any form of analytics. We do not track your behaviour, usage patterns, or sessions.

5. Third-Party Services

Affordly uses the following third-party service solely to load fonts:

  • Google Fonts — loads the Montserrat font. Google may log the font request as per their own privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

No other third-party services, SDKs, advertising networks, or analytics platforms are used.

6. Advertisements

Affordly contains no advertisements of any kind. We do not work with any advertising networks or allow any third party to serve ads within the app or website.

7. Children's Privacy

Affordly does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since we collect no data at all, the app is safe for all ages.

8. Data Security

Since all data remains on your device and is never transmitted, there is no risk of a server-side data breach affecting your information. Your data is as secure as your own device.

9. Your Rights

Since we store no data about you on our end, there is nothing for us to delete, export, or correct on your behalf. You can delete all locally stored data at any time by:

  • Tapping "Clear all" in the History section of Affordly
  • Clearing your browser's site data in browser settings
  • Uninstalling the Android app

10. Changes to This Policy

If we make any changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of Affordly after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
